# Results

---

# Result 1

## Angular convention verification

After correcting the definition of

\[
\cos\theta,
\]

the concurrence peak now appears at

\[
\cos\theta \approx -0.51,
\]

in agreement with

Durupt et al. (2026).

---

# Result 2

## Energy dependence of concurrence

As the collision energy increases,

- the concurrence maximum increases,

- the peak shifts toward

\[
\cos\theta\approx -0.5,
\]

- the peak position becomes nearly stable at high energies.

---

# Result 3

## Purity

Purity exhibits a different qualitative behaviour.

At

350–365 GeV

the distribution is almost monotonic.

Starting from approximately

500 GeV,

a clear peak develops.

This suggests a qualitative transition between

365 GeV

and

500 GeV.

---

# Result 4

## EOF and Negativity

EOF and Negativity follow the same qualitative trend as concurrence.

The three observables remain highly correlated over all investigated energies.
