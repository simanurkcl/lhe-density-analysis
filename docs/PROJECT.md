# Simulation and Analysis of Quantum Entanglement in Lepton Collisions Using Spin Density Matrices

## Project Overview

This project investigates quantum entanglement in high-energy lepton collisions using spin density matrices and quantum information observables.

Unlike traditional collider analyses, which mainly focus on kinematic quantities such as cross sections, invariant masses, and angular distributions, this project studies the quantum information carried by the produced particles.

The ultimate goal is to understand how different quantum information observables characterize the quantum state of the produced system and whether these observables may provide complementary information for future particle physics analyses.

---

# Motivation

Quantum information theory has recently become an important research direction in high-energy physics.

Modern theoretical developments have shown that collider events contain much more information than classical observables alone. Spin density matrices preserve the complete spin information of the produced particles, allowing quantum correlations and entanglement to be studied directly.

Lepton colliders provide an especially clean environment for these studies because the initial state is precisely known, making them ideal laboratories for investigating quantum properties.

---

# Research Question

How do different quantum information observables characterize quantum entanglement in high-energy lepton collisions, and how do these observables behave under different physical conditions?

---

# Hypothesis

Different quantum information observables do not necessarily provide identical physical information.

Although some observables are mathematically related, others may respond differently under varying collision energies, beam polarizations, detector cuts, and experimental conditions.

A systematic comparison of these observables may reveal which quantities are the most informative and robust for studying quantum entanglement in collider physics.

---

# Objectives

The objectives of this project are:

- Understand the physical meaning of spin density matrices.
- Simulate lepton collisions using MadGraph5 Density Mode.
- Extract spin density matrices event-by-event.
- Calculate quantum information observables.
- Study correlations between different observables.
- Investigate their dependence on kinematic variables.
- Compare their behavior under different physical conditions.
- Explore their possible applications in future collider experiments.
